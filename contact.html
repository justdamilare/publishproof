Email damilare@srevana.com for account access, licence activation, purchase, or product questions. For an order question, include the email used at checkout and the order reference if available. Do not send a licence key by email.

To report a potential security issue, follow the contact instructions in our security policy. Please do not include sensitive customer or campaign material in an initial report.

For a request about an optional PublishProof account or synced records, email damilare@srevana.com from the account email address and state the request clearly. Product terms and privacy information are available in the Licence & privacy page.
