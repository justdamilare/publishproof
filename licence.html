These terms describe the PublishProof founding agency licence. They supplement the checkout and payment terms presented by Lemon Squeezy, the merchant of record.

Free trial

You may create one publication record to evaluate the workflow. The trial does not grant ongoing commercial agency use.

Commercial licence

A completed one-time purchase grants the purchasing agency a perpetual, non-exclusive and non-transferable licence to use PublishProof for its own work and client work. The licence permits unlimited publication records and up to five simultaneous browser or device activations within that agency.

You may deactivate an installation and reuse that activation elsewhere. You may not resell, sublicense, copy, reverse engineer, or make the service available as a competing product. A receipt and valid licence key are proof of entitlement.

Updates and availability

The founding licence includes workflow updates made available during the first 12 months after purchase. The version available at the end of that period remains licensed. PublishProof is provided as a practical recordkeeping aid, without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability or that it determines legal compliance.

Privacy and local storage

Publication records and selected files are not uploaded to PublishProof. Records are stored in the browser’s local storage. Files are processed locally to create a SHA-256 fingerprint; only the fingerprint is retained in the record.

Licence activation sends the licence key, the checkout email address, and a non-personal browser-instance label to Lemon Squeezy for verification. Checkout, tax and receipt data are processed by Lemon Squeezy under its own terms. Clearing browser data may remove records and the local activation, so customers should export JSON backups regularly.
