About PublishProof

A record for the
decision behind the post.

PublishProof is built by Srevana for agency teams that need a straightforward trail from AI-assisted production to client delivery or publication.

AboutPurposePrinciplesContact

Purpose

AI tools can make assets quickly. The remaining professional work is documenting what changed, who reviewed the final output, what the audience needs to know, and what was actually delivered. PublishProof helps teams keep that record while the facts are still clear.

How the product is designed

  • Local first: selected media files are fingerprinted in the browser and are not uploaded.
  • Explicit sync: an optional account can sync structured records only after the user enables it.
  • Source linked: Article 50 workflows point to primary guidance rather than produce a compliance score.
  • Portable: teams can export records for clients, internal files, or later handoffs.
