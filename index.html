Before you purchase

Clear answers,
before checkout.

What happens after payment?

Lemon Squeezy emails your receipt and licence key. The welcome page can activate the browser immediately, and the same key can activate up to five browsers or devices.

Do I need an account?

No. The free record and licensed local workspace work without an account. An optional secure account can synchronise records between devices without removing local-only mode.

Where are my records stored?

By default, structured records stay in this browser. If you explicitly enable optional account sync, encrypted transport sends the structured record to EU-hosted account storage. PublishProof never uploads the selected media file; it calculates and stores only a SHA-256 fingerprint.

Is this legal advice?

No. PublishProof is a conservative, source-linked workflow and recordkeeping aid. Your organisation remains responsible for its legal assessment and publication decisions.
